Important Info

The Rule (this part is real): A law called the Music Modernization Act says something wild. If royalty money sits unclaimed for too long, it doesn't just disappear or sit there forever. The law says that cash should go to whoever already sells the most music. So if you skip registering your songs, the rules push your money toward the biggest labels instead of you. That's not us guessing — it's written right into the actual law. (Source: Chartlex)

The Number: The group holding this leftover cash is called The MLC. In 2021 alone, they received $424,384,787 in unclaimed royalties from streaming services. That's the biggest one-time payout to songwriters in U.S. history. Real number, real check, real money just sitting there. (Source: The MLC / Chartlex)

The Fair Part: To be fair, The MLC has paid out almost $4 billion since 2021, and most of it lands with the right person eventually. This isn't a scam. Key Terms

PRO: (Performance Rights Agency) A group that tracks when songs play on radio, TV, or in stores. They collect money from those places and pay the songwriters. (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, AllTrack)

MLC: (The Mechanical Licensing Collective) A special organization that collects money from apps like Spotify and Apple Music to pay the people who wrote the songs. They hold the Black Box Bank.

Registered Song: Filling out a form with your PRO to list your song's details, so they know who wrote what and can send them the money.

Splits: A simple agreement that shows what percentage of the song's money each writer gets to keep.

Music Publishing: The business of taking care of song ideas, protecting them, and finding ways to make money from them.

Royalty Collection: Gathering up all the small payments earned when music is played or sold, then paying the creators.

Mechanical Rights: Permission to copy and play a song on CDs, vinyl records, or streaming apps.

Publishing Rights: Ownership of the written song—the melody and words—rather than the actual sound recording.

Performance Rights: Permission to play a song out loud in public, like on the radio, at concerts, or in stores.

Composition & Lyrics Roles: Selecting on your PRO form who made the tunes and words, so the song's writers get proper credit.

Performer Roles: Selecting on your PRO form who sang or played instruments on the song, so the right artists get named.

Production & Engineering Roles: Selecting on your PRO form the studio helpers who recorded and mixed the song, making sure every team member gets credit.

What is Black Box
